**@Trader_XO’s VWAP Framework (Compiled Brief)**

**Core Rule**  
VWAP (weekly + monthly) acts as the primary dynamic pivot/mean for bias and inflection.  
- **Bearish bias**: Price below both weekly & monthly VWAP → favor shorts, rotations lower, fade rallies.  
- **Bullish bias**: Price above both → favor longs, continuation higher.  
- **Neutral/mixed**: Price between them or split across the two.

**Acceptance Filter** (confirms the break is real)  
Not just a wick/cross — requires **sustained acceptance** on the new side:  
- Multiple closes beyond the VWAP (or key level).  
- Successful retest (holds as new support/resistance) or failed probes/auctions the other way.  
- “Losing and staying below” or “acceptance back above” strengthens the directional move.  
Quick reversals = potential fakeout/range behavior.

**Confluence Layer** (multiplies conviction)  
VWAP gains power when stacked with:  
- Value areas / POC / previous month’s highs-lows.  
- Local resistance/support or range boundaries.  
- 12/25 EMAs or HTF bands (shaped in the same direction).  
- Order-flow clues (stacked bids/asks, absorption near the level).  

**High-Probability Setup Pattern**  
Bias (e.g., below both VWAPs) + strong confluence zone + decisive break + acceptance → directional trade with follow-through.  
Example: Shift above local resistance + weekly VWAP + monthly VWAP = bullish momentum odds improve.

This is the repeatable mechanical core he applies before discretionary judgment (tape, context, position management). Use it as a bias filter + shift trigger, then layer your own confluence checks for best results.
